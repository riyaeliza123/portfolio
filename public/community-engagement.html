Overview

Outside of my core work, I am actively involved in the local tech and business communities. I have organized and supported numerous tech meetups and conferences, collaborated with groups such as Google Developer Groups, Tech2Step, BC India Business Network and regularly mentor and volunteer to help make data science more accessible. I've also spoken at organizations including Google Developers and DataCan, sharing applied lessons from my work and supporting community-driven learning.

Dive into Data Science: A Beginner's Guide

In 2023, I conducted a workshop at Kaggle titled "Dive into Data Science: A Beginner's Guide" along with a Computer Vision project Demo. The workshop aimed to provide mentorship to KaggleX participants, helping them grasp the fundamentals of the data science process and, most importantly, guiding them on how to embark on their data science learning journey.
